Writing

I write books about the world's greatest progressive rock band, Yes.

I have researched, written and published two Amazon Bestselling books and colour supplements so far about my favourite band.

Yes - The Tormato Story won a certificate of merit in the 2024 Association for Recorded Sound Collections Awards for Excellence in Historical Research, was chosen as one of the Prog Magazine Critics' Choices in 2023 and was shortlisted for a Selfie Award in 2024.

“A fascinating and almost forensic study into an album often overlooked by Yesfans – after reading you’ll never hear the album in the same way again!” 

Geoff Bailie, The Prog Report www.progreport.com on Yes - The Tormato Story

"Superb book , full of information about the Yes masterpiece which is still to this day a controversial album with many fans, this book is a treasure trove of info and will help and inform fans as to the stories and ideas behind the songs, highly recommended."

Mark Johnson, Amazon review of Tales From Topographic Oceans - Yes Album Listening Guide
