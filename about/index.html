About

I am Kevin Mulryne. I live in Stratford-upon-Avon, UK, with my wife, Sarah, and our mad rescue English Springer Spaniel, Samson. Our son William also lives here when he isn't out photographing, working in gardens or helping to manage a smallholding near Evesham. Sometimes, our daughter, Charlotte, is here in breaks from her Coventry University degree in songwriting. Our other son, Edward, lives in California with his wife, Sahar. They are both actors and playwrights.
